Waterline

A personal budgeting tool for staying afloat between paydays.

Waterline works out the "waterline": how much money needs to stay in the bank to cover the bills and planned spending due before the next paycheck. It tracks scheduled bills, everyday spending, savings set aside for larger expenses, and debt, and sorts transactions into categories.

Personal use only. Waterline is built and used by one person to manage their own accounts. It is not offered to the public: there are no sign-ups, no customers and no other users.

How it connects to banks

Waterline uses Plaid to read transactions from the owner's own bank and card accounts. The connection is read-only: Waterline cannot move money, make payments or change anything at the bank.

Where the data lives

Transactions are stored in a database on the owner's own computer, not on a server. Plaid connection keys are kept in the computer's secure keychain. Nothing is sold or shared. The privacy policy has the details.
